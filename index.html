Theoretical Brief

What proof of work is actually doing, in one image: a uniform distribution over hash space, and the constraint that carves information out of it.

SHA-256 outputs are uniform and equiprobable across a 2256 state space. Counting consecutive leading zeros imposes a constraint on that space — and each zero is a bit of uncertainty removed.
